About the Mission

Seeds of Change is a digital advocacy platform dedicated to urban gardening. We believe that by reclaiming vacant spaces, we can solve critical societal issues like food insecurity and social isolation.

Our gardens act as "outdoor classrooms" where local youth learn about sustainability, and neighbors build lasting bonds through shared labor and harvests.
